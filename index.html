Geograph cold spots

Choose a folder to start.

What this does. Reads the GPS position in each photograph, works out its 100 m Ordnance Survey (or Irish Grid) square, asks Geograph how many pictures already exist there, and shows the emptiest first. For a photograph you want to contribute it can ask a vision model to draft a title and description, then submit it to Geograph.

Nothing is written to the photo folder. Your decisions are kept in this browser; use “Save state…” to keep a copy elsewhere. Set up the vision model and your Geograph upload token under Settings first.

Where is the subject?

Geograph files a picture under the square of what it shows. Blue ring: camera (line: the direction it faced, where the phone recorded it). Pin: subject — click the map or drag the pin, or click a green Geograph picture or an orange OpenStreetMap feature to use its position. The red box is the subject's 100 m square.
